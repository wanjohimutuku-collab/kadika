# ADR 042: Charging for generated cards and reminders

Status: accepted. API, database, console, staff panel, public pricing page and tests are built.
Enforcement is **off** by default.

## Context

Billing was a manual flag (ADR 012): staff marked an event `free` or `paid`, and nothing limited what an
event used. The business makes its money from **card generation**: hosts pay per card, cheaper the more
they buy, and each card carries reminder messages (open decision 5 in CLAUDE.md §15).

## Decision

1. **What is charged: a generated card.** A guest's card is generated the first time it is **sent** (SMS
   or WhatsApp) or **printed**; that sets `guests.card_generated_at`. Sending or printing it again, or
   both, is free. Adding guests to the list costs nothing. A generated card stays counted after its guest is
   deleted. Cards already sent before this ADR are back-filled as generated by the migration.
2. **Prices** (`pricing_settings`, one row, shape in `@harusi/schemas/billing`): a price per card by order
   size, from the business: **1–99 cards TZS 2,400, 100–499 TZS 2,200, 500 and more TZS 1,950** (500
   exactly is the cheapest tier). The tier is set by the quantity in one order. Every card bought adds
   **4 reminder credits**; one credit is one SMS or one WhatsApp message. **Reminder packs** add credits
   without cards. Every event gets a free allowance (10 cards, 40 credits). KES prices, the free allowance
   and the packs are assumptions until confirmed. Staff with the `plan` permission edit all of it at
   `/ops/billing`; the public sees it at `/bei` (`GET /v1/pricing`).
3. **What spends credits:** every SMS or WhatsApp message that is not the card itself and did not fail —
   reminders, pledge reminders. The card's own delivery (`invite`, the WhatsApp→SMS `fallback`, `test`)
   never does. Failed messages are not charged.
4. **Printed cards.** Each guest has `print_card` (default: yes when the guest has no phone). The host
   chooses many at once from the guest list (`POST /v1/events/:id/guests/print-card`) or per guest in the
   form, and generates them in bulk on Kadi (`POST /v1/events/:id/cards/pdf` with `marked: true`), or
   prints a hand-picked selection from the guest list. A print that would generate more new cards than are
   left is refused whole.
5. **Orders** (`event_purchases`): the owner buys any number of cards, or a pack, on the event's Plan page.
   The quantity, unit price, credits and amount are copied onto the row, so later price changes never alter
   an order. Only `paid` rows count.
   - **Pay by phone**: a mobile-money prompt through the gateway of ADR 036, reference `HB…`. The payment
     webhook asks billing first and falls back to pledges; a reference is applied once. Credit follows
     what the gateway says it took: a short amount leaves the order pending for staff. Pending orders are
     reconciled on the worker tick.
   - **Bank or cash**: pending until staff confirm it with a note (`ops.purchase_paid`). Staff can also
     **grant** cards or credits with a reason (`ops.grant`).
   - A paid card order also sets `events.plan = 'paid'` for the existing dashboards.
6. **Enforcement** (only with `enforced` on): a send or print that would generate more cards, or spend
   more credits, than remain is refused up front with HTTP **402 `payment_required`** and
   `details: { reason: 'quota_exceeded', resource: 'cards' | 'messages', remaining, needed }`. During a run
   each new card and each reminder takes from a per-run budget, refunded when nothing reached the guest.
   The console turns a 402 into a toast linking to the Plan page.
7. **Revenue**: the staff overview shows 30-day revenue from paid orders per currency (grants excluded)
   and flags bank or cash orders waiting for confirmation.

## Consequences

- Turning enforcement on affects every event at once. Events already past their allowance see 0 left and
  need cards or a staff grant; grant existing paid events first.
- Two sends running at the same moment can each see the same remaining credit and together exceed it by
  up to one send's size. Acceptable for v1; a row lock on the event would close it.
- The mobile app has no buying screen yet: owners buy from the web console, and the app shows the 402.
