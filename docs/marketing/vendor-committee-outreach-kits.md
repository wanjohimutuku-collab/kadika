## Partner Tracking Sheet Template

```csv
partner,category,city,contact_date,status,events_referred,reward_status,notes
"",decorator|mc|venue|photographer|kitchen_party|faith_coordinator,"Dar es Salaam|Arusha|Mwanza|Nairobi",YYYY-MM-DD,contacted|replied|demo_sent|active|paused,0,pending|credited|n_a,""
```
