> **Status (2026-11-04):** the `robots.txt` block (§3), the blocked logo, the missing `og:image`, the shared Organization entity, `/bei` and `/programu` schema, the `/maswali` headings and the `llms.txt` guides are fixed in code, not yet deployed. See [SEO-AUDIT.md](SEO-AUDIT.md) for what changed and what is still open.

Audit date: 2026-11-04. Method: raw HTML fetched without JavaScript (as a non-rendering AI crawler sees it), using an `OAI-SearchBot` user agent, for 16 pages: home (sw, en), `/ke`, `/bei`, `/maswali`, `/kuhusu` and 9 guide and product pages. Also checked: `robots.txt`, `/llms.txt`, the sitemap index and its 3 child sitemaps (64 URLs), response headers, and one web search for off-site brand mentions. Repo source checked: `apps/web/app/robots.ts`, `src/components/public/content-page.tsx`, `src/content/metadata.ts`.

Not measured: actual AI citations, Search Console data, ranking positions. The platform scores below are therefore qualitative.

Framing (Google AI optimization guide): optimising for AI search surfaces is SEO. Every finding here is an SEO fundamental applied to AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity and Copilot.

## 3. AI crawler access status

The live `robots.txt` (generated by `apps/web/app/robots.ts`) has two groups with the same private list: one naming 7 search bots, and `*` for everyone else.

**Critical defect.** Robots rules are prefix matches, and Google applies the longest matching rule. `Disallow: /k` (length 2) beats `Allow: /` (length 1) for **every path starting with `/k`**, not just the card-number page `/k`. `Disallow: /en/k` does the same under `/en`. Of the 64 sitemap URLs, **34 are blocked for every crawler**:

- `/kadi-ya-harusi-mtandaoni` (the main product page), `/kadi-za-mwaliko-mtandaoni`, `/kadi-ya-send-off`, `/kadi-ya-uchumba`, `/kadi-ya-kuzaliwa`, `/kadi-ya-kitchen-party`, `/kadi-ya-mahafali`, `/kadi-za-mwaliko-biashara`, `/kadi-ya-harusi-bila-smartphone`, `/kadi-ya-mtandaoni-vs-karatasi`, `/kutuma-kadi-za-mwaliko-whatsapp`, `/kusimamia-majibu-ya-rsvp`, `/kuhusu`
- the same 13 pages under `/en/`
- `/ke` and all 7 Kenya pages

`Disallow: /icons/` also blocks `/icons/512.png`, the logo named in the Organization and Article publisher schema.

`/k` already sends `noindex` (`apps/web/app/k/page.tsx`), so the disallow is not needed to keep it out of results. In fact, blocking it stops crawlers from seeing that `noindex`.

Add a unit test that every sitemap URL is allowed by the generated rules, so a short prefix can't do this again.

## 4. llms.txt status

Present at `/llms.txt`: title, a clear one-paragraph description, 6 links and a trust line. Well formed.

- It links to `/ke`, which `robots.txt` blocks.
- It lists none of the guides.
- Google states `llms.txt` does not help or hurt Search or AI Overviews, and no major AI provider has confirmed using it. It carries **no weight** in the score above. Keep it, but don't invest more than adding the guides.

## 6. Passage-level citability

Strong blocks already in place (quote-ready, self-contained):

- `/kadi-ya-harusi-bila-smartphone`, short answer: guest gets an SMS with their short number (e.g. KH-4826) or a paper card with the same QR; at the gate they give the number, name or last 4 phone digits, and the committee admits them without internet.
- `/kadi-ya-harusi-mtandaoni`, H2 "Kadi ya harusi mtandaoni ni nini?" ("What is an online wedding card?"): a real definition section.
- `/usalama-wa-kadi-za-mtandaoni`: a clear list of what a genuine card never asks for (password, PIN, OTP, app or attachment). This is the most distinctive passage on the site and directly answers a common fear.
- Home FAQ answers on price (TZS 2,400 per card, falling to TZS 1,950 from 500 cards; first 5 cards free) and offline gate behaviour.

Weak spots:

- **Home**: the H1 is a slogan; the first 60 words are slogan, buttons, then demo-card text ("Asha & Juma… Bi. Mwanaidi Salim… KH-4826"). AI systems weight the first 30% of a page; nothing there says what Kadi Sasa *is*.
- **Guides are short** (336 to 405 words). Each answers one question well but has few specifics an answer engine can't get elsewhere.
- **No figures or sources**: no numbers on feature-phone share, gate time or delivery rates, and no links to primary sources (TCRA, the Personal Data Protection Act).
- **`/bei`**: no short answer, no date, no schema; prices are in cards only.

## 7. Server-side rendering check

**Pass.** All tested pages return their full text, headings, FAQ answers and JSON-LD in the initial HTML; nothing depends on JavaScript. Non-rendering crawlers (GPTBot, PerplexityBot) see the same content as Googlebot.

Secondary: public pages respond `Cache-Control: private, no-cache, no-store`, so Cloudflare serves every crawler hit from the origin. The marketing pages appear to render dynamically (the public layout reads request headers). Making them static or ISR would cut TTFB for users and crawlers alike. Not an access blocker.

## 8. Top 5 highest-impact changes

1. **Fix `robots.txt` (critical, about 10 minutes).** `/k` → `/k$`, `/en/k` → `/en/k$`; unblock the logo; add the sitemap-vs-robots test. This restores crawl access to the main product page, the About page and the whole Kenya edition. Then resubmit the sitemap in Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
2. **Build off-site entity presence.** Google Business Profile, a LinkedIn company page, an Instagram account and a short Swahili YouTube demo (card on a phone, gate scan offline), then list them in Organization `sameAs`. Seed honest JamiiForums and Facebook group answers from pilot committees. This is the largest gap against M-Kadi.
3. **Publish original data from the pilots.** The pilot metrics already defined in CLAUDE.md §14 (share of guests reached, share served by SMS / feature phones, median gate time per guest, first-scan success rate) are exactly the unique, quotable numbers answer engines look for. One "Kadi za harusi Tanzania: tulichojifunza kutoka harusi X" page with a dated table would be the most citable page on the site.
4. **Add images and `og:image`.** Every page has `twitter:card=summary` and no `og:image`. Add per-page Open Graph images (`opengraph-image.tsx`) and real screenshots (card on a phone, gate result, michango report) with descriptive Swahili alt text. This also improves WhatsApp link previews, the main way these pages get shared.
5. **Name a person and keep pages fresh.** Add a named author or reviewer (founder or support lead) with a short bio on `/kuhusu` and `Person` schema; only bump `dateModified` when content actually changes, and review each guide quarterly.

## 9. Schema recommendations

Current: home has `Organization`, `WebSite`, `Service`, `FAQPage`; guides have `Article` or `WebPage`, `BreadcrumbList`, `FAQPage`; `/bei` has none.

- **Organization** (`apps/web/src/components/site/home-page.tsx` and `content-page.tsx`): add `sameAs` (profiles from change 2), `contactPoint` (committee support phone or WhatsApp, `availableLanguage: ["sw","en"]`), `foundingLocation` or `address` (Tanzania), and use one `@id` (`https://kadisasa.com/#org`) everywhere instead of the inline duplicate `Organization` objects on guide pages. Point `logo` at an unblocked URL.
- **`/bei`**: `Service` or `Product` with `offers` as an `AggregateOffer` (`priceCurrency: "TZS"`, `lowPrice: 1950`, `highPrice: 2400`) or one `Offer` per tier with `UnitPriceSpecification`. Keep the figures generated from the same pricing source as the page.
- **`/programu`**: `SoftwareApplication` (`operatingSystem: "Android"`, `applicationCategory: "LifestyleApplication"`, `offers` price 0).
- **Articles**: `author` as `Person` (with `url` to the bio) and `publisher` as the `#org` reference; a separate `datePublished`.
- **FAQPage**: keep it (it gives machines clean Q&A pairs), but Google shows FAQ rich results only for government and health sites, so expect no visual change.
- Avoid `HowTo` (deprecated in Google results).

## 10. Content reformatting suggestions

1. **Home, first 60 words (sw).** Add a plain definition right under the H1, before the demo card. Draft for a native speaker to review: "Kadi Sasa ni huduma ya Kitanzania ya kadi za mwaliko mtandaoni. Kila mgeni anapata kadi yake yenye QR na namba fupi kwa WhatsApp, SMS au karatasi; kamati inaona majibu na michango, na lango linaingiza wageni hata bila mtandao." Mirror it on `/en` and `/ke`. Move the demo card after it, or make it a figure with a caption.
2. **`/bei`**: open with a short answer ("Kadi 5 za kwanza ni bure. Baada ya hapo TZS 2,400 kwa kadi, hadi TZS 1,950 kwa kadi 500 au zaidi."), then render the tiers as a real `<table>` (Idadi ya kadi | Bei kwa kadi | Mfano), and show an updated date.
3. **`/maswali`**: drop the duplicate H2; put each question in an `<h3>` inside its `<summary>`; group into H2 sections (Gharama, Usalama, Michango, Siku ya tukio).
4. **`/kadi-ya-mtandaoni-vs-karatasi`**: make the comparison a table (cost per guest, delivery time, works without smartphone, RSVP tracking, gate check-in, risk of forged copies).
5. **Guides**: add one concrete, dated, sourced fact per guide (e.g. feature-phone share from TCRA statistics on `/kadi-ya-harusi-bila-smartphone`), and one worked example ("Harusi ya wageni 300: …") to lift them from about 350 to about 700 useful words.
6. **`/en/` slugs** are Swahili (`/en/kadi-ya-harusi-mtandaoni`). Acceptable, but English-language queries match better on English slugs; consider it when the English edition grows. Also, `/ke/wedding-contributions-guide` is listed as the `en-KE` alternate of the TZ product page `/michango-ya-harusi`; hreflang should only pair equivalent pages.
